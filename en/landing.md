## What you will make

In this project, you'll code a side-on fighting game where a stickman hero punches, kicks, slashes, and rolls to survive wave after wave of giant dinosaurs stomping through Tokyo.

Have a play with the finished game to see what you'll make. Use the arrow keys to walk and jump, then use `space`, `m`, `n`, and `v` to fight.
