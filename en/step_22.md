## Choose a strike colour

The bright colour on the fighter's fist, foot, or weapon will tell an enemy that it has been hit.

<h2 class="c-project-heading--explainer">What you need to do</h2>

![player sprite](images/player.png)

Stop the project and click on the `player` sprite.

Open the `Costumes`{:class="block3looks"} tab and choose a punch, kick, or sword costume that shows the bright colour on the strike.

This is the **strike colour**.

Choose a colour that appears nowhere else on the fighter or Stage, so it can only mean an attack.

## Now run your code

Remember where the colour appears. You'll make the enemy sense it next.
